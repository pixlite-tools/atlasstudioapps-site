RedBal Privacy Policy

App-specific information about records, permissions and the current internal-test release.

This notice describes RedBal 0.6.1 (build 9), including its health features. Publication of this notice does not mean that the app is publicly released or approved by Google Play.

Source notice effective: 26 September 2026. Published on this website: 2 November 2026.

RedBal, provided by Atlasova Studio, organizes reminders, health schedules, family profiles and small-business expiry inventory. No RedBal account is required.

Local records The app stores reminder titles, dates, notes, places and preferences on your device. My Family stores names/labels, relationships and links to records. My Health stores medication names, user-entered dose text, schedules, taken/skipped history, remaining-dose counts and appointment details. My Shop stores product names, barcodes, lots, quantities, expiry dates, optional costs/currency and stock movements.

RedBal does not intentionally upload these records to a RedBal server or sell them. They use Android app-private storage; the current version does not add a separate app password or application-level database encryption. Protect your phone with a screen lock.

Barcode scanning and third-party diagnostics Google Code Scanner through Google Play services performs scanning on the device without RedBal requesting CAMERA permission. Google documents that it does not store scanner images or decoded results. Ordinary barcodes identify products; only explicit supported GS1 fields can prefill a date or lot for review.

On-device image processing does not mean that the SDK sends no technical data. Google's ML Kit documentation describes collection of device and app information, device/per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input/output sizes, feature versions, event types and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. These technical data may be processed by Google depending on SDK version and configuration. Google documents HTTPS transport and no onward third-party sharing for the listed ML Kit data. RedBal does not enable the optional scanner auto-zoom feature.

See Google's documentation: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/vision/barcode-scanning/code-scanner. Google Play services also operates under Google's applicable privacy terms.

Notifications and device permissions RedBal requests notification permission for your selected alerts and boot access to restore schedules. It does not request location or contacts permissions. You can disable notifications in the app or Android settings.

In version 0.6.1/build 9, health notifications use generic text without the person, medication, dose or doctor name, and request hidden lock-screen content. Taken/Skip changes are made inside My Health rather than from notification buttons. Other reminders can contain the titles you enter; review your device notification settings.

Backup and device transfers Cloud backup is disabled in the app configuration. Version 0.6.1/build 9 also explicitly excludes app data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer rules. Older builds and device manufacturers may behave differently. These settings are not a recovery service: deleting or losing local app data can permanently lose your records.

Advertising and payments Advertising and Business Pro purchases are disabled in builds 8 and 9. There is no active Paddle/external checkout link in this Play release. We will update this policy and the Google Play disclosures before enabling advertising or paid features.

Retention and deletion Records remain until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall. When removing a family profile, you choose either to move linked records to Me or to delete the profile and its linked records. Device-level deletion does not constitute a promise of forensic secure erasure. For Google SDK diagnostic retention and controls, consult Google's applicable policies.

Health and children RedBal is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or determine doses. Follow qualified healthcare professionals' instructions. It is a general utility, not specifically directed to children.
