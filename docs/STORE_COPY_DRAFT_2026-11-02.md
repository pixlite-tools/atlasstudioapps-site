## PixLite — English listing proposal

Title: PixLite

Short description:
Scan documents, create PDFs, resize images and make QR codes on Android.

Description:
Bring everyday document tasks together in PixLite.

- Scan documents with Google Play services.
- Create a PDF from selected images.
- Combine multiple images into a PDF.
- Resize images and adjust JPEG compression.
- Create a QR code from text or a link.
- Find generated files in local history and choose when to share them.
- Switch between English, French, Spanish and Arabic.

Document content is processed on your device. User-initiated sharing passes the selected file to the app you choose. Google SDKs and advertising have their own technical data practices; see the privacy notice for details.

Release checks before using this copy:
Verify native gallery/camera/scanner and receiving-app sharing on the final release. Confirm minimum Android version, final permissions, production ads and consent, and release-specific privacy/Data Safety disclosures. QA3's disabled ads do not verify production consent.

## RedBal — English listing proposal

Title: RedBal

Short description:
Reminders for dates, family schedules and shop stock expiry, in one place.

Description:
Keep everyday dates and expiry information organized with RedBal.

- Add a reminder with a date, notes and alert offsets.
- Search records and view upcoming dates.
- Organize linked records with family profiles.
- Track product batches, quantities and expiry dates in My Shop.
- Use barcode scanning to identify a product. A normal product barcode does not supply its expiry date.
- Use English, French, Spanish or Arabic.

RedBal 0.6.1 keeps records on your device and does not require a RedBal account. The current release has no active advertising or Business Pro purchase flow. Android notification permissions and device settings affect reminder delivery.
