Common questions

Where can I download the apps?

RedBal is in internal testing. PixLite and Atlas: Moroccan Darija are in preparation. Public store links will be added to the app pages when available. We do not distribute APK files from this website.

Do I need a RedBal account?

No account is required in RedBal 0.6.1. Records are stored on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it can remove your records.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Check notification permission, the in-app notification setting, device time and battery restrictions. Notification delivery depends on Android and device settings. Include your device model when reporting a problem.

Can support recover my local documents or reminders?

We do not operate a recovery service for local app files or RedBal records. Keep copies of important files in a location you control. Never rely on a reminder app for urgent or safety-critical alerts.

How do I report a privacy issue?

Email us with the app name and a description. Redact names, account information and document contents from screenshots. Read the relevant privacy notice before sending files.
